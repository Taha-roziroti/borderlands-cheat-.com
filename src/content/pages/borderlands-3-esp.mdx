# Borderlands 3 ESP for Enemies, Bosses and Loot

Exploring Pandora means dealing with more than what's directly in front of you. Enemies can appear from different directions, powerful bosses bring their own challenges and valuable equipment can be scattered throughout an encounter.

Borderlands 3 ESP brings this information into a customizable visual interface, giving you more control over what appears on screen.

The feature set covers enemy information, boss indicators, loot rarity, weapon and gear drops, mission objectives and radar. Each category serves a different purpose, making the overall interface adaptable to your preferences.

## Borderlands 3 Enemy ESP

Enemy ESP provides visual information about enemies and mobs during gameplay.

The available settings include enemy highlights, health bars and distance indicators. These elements make the information associated with each enemy easier to read at a glance.

You can also use specialized markers for elite enemies and bosses. Weak-point highlighting adds another useful visual element by drawing attention to important enemy locations.

Rather than relying on one generic display, the Borderlands 3 ESP menu offers several individual options that can be configured around your preferred interface.

## Boss Markers and Health Bar Display

Boss encounters are some of the most memorable parts of Borderlands 3. Their size, health and attack patterns make them stand out from standard enemy encounters.

The ESP feature set includes dedicated elite and boss markers, alongside health-bar information and distance indicators.

Weak-point highlighting adds further detail, helping distinguish important target areas from the rest of the enemy display.

These visual elements are particularly useful when configuring a cleaner, more informative interface for combat.

## Loot Rarity ESP and Item Filters

Finding equipment is a huge part of the Borderlands experience. Weapons, gear and other drops are constantly changing as you explore different areas.

Loot rarity ESP helps organize the information surrounding available drops. The menu also includes gear and weapon drop filters, allowing you to customize which items appear in the visual display.

Additional markers cover chests and caches, making exploration information easier to organize.

## Radar and Mission Objective Markers

The 2D radar provides another way to organize information around your character.

Its available settings include:

- Player markers.
- Adjustable radar range.
- Radar zoom.
- Mission objective markers.
- Chest and cache markers.

The ability to adjust radar range and zoom gives you control over how much surrounding information appears in the interface.

Mission markers add another layer of organization, especially when moving between objectives and exploration areas.
