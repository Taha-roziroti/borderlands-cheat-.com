# Borderlands 3 Aimbot and Soft Aim Features

Borderlands 3 has no shortage of intense encounters. Between fast-moving enemies, powerful bosses and crowded combat areas, maintaining control over your aim can make a big difference to your gameplay.

The Borderlands 3 aimbot feature set is built around customizable soft aim. Instead of relying on one fixed targeting configuration, you can adjust the available settings to create an experience that matches your preferences.

From FOV and smoothing to target selection and key assignments, the available options give you detailed control over how the feature is configured.

## Customizable Soft Aim Settings

The foundation of the Borderlands 3 soft aim system is its collection of adjustable settings.

Enable or disable soft aim, choose your preferred activation key and configure the targeting FOV. These settings let you define the area in which targets can be considered.

Aim smoothing provides another layer of customization, while maximum aim distance allows you to define the distance used by the targeting configuration.

You can also enable visible checks and choose whether teammates or allies should be ignored.

## Adjust Aim FOV and Smoothing

FOV and smoothing are two of the most important settings in a customized aiming configuration.

Aim FOV determines the size of the targeting area. A smaller field creates a more focused selection area, while a wider field covers a larger section of the screen.

Aim smoothing controls the transition of the aiming movement. Adjusting it lets you personalize the feel of the soft aim feature.

The interface also includes a Draw FOV option, giving you a visual representation of the configured area while setting up your preferences.

## Head, Chest and Closest Target Selection

Different situations call for different target preferences.

The Borderlands 3 aimbot includes three bone-selection options:

- **Head:** Prioritizes head targeting.
- **Chest:** Uses chest targeting.
- **Closest:** Selects the closest available target bone.

These choices make the configuration more flexible across different encounters.

You can combine target selection with your preferred FOV, smoothing and activation-key settings to create a setup that feels consistent with your individual preferences.

## Visible Checks and Target Indicators

The additional visual settings provide useful feedback while configuring soft aim.

Visible checks add a visibility condition to target selection. Draw Target Line provides a visual indicator connecting the aiming point with the selected target.

You can also display the FOV boundary, adjust maximum aim distance and configure teammate or ally filtering.

Together, these options make the soft aim configuration easier to understand and personalize.

## Configure Your Aim Preferences

The Borderlands 3 menu includes a save and load configuration system, allowing you to keep your preferred setup available.
